I'm Jessica Emad Fawzy, a UI/UX Designer and recent graduate from the Faculty of Computers and Information, Tanta University — Class of 2025. I specialize in the full UX process: from research, empathy mapping, persona creation, competitive analysis, and problem framing (HMW) all the way to wireframes, high-fidelity prototypes, and polished Figma presentations. I believe great design is invisible: it simply feels right. Whether it's a children's gaming app or a fashion e-commerce platform, I bring empathy, creativity, and precision to every pixel.

"Design is not just what it looks like — design is how it works."
